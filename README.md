Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479).

Two lines: line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence built on a sunbathing analogy; line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works — what it maps and what it leaves out.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A toaster is a tiny sunbathing bed for bread, where two glowing bars tan both sides at once until the bread pops up like it has had enough of the sun.
> It works because heat browns bread the way the sun browns skin, but it leaves out that the sun never ejects you with a spring when you are done.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
